CONSULTANTS / ADVISORS / THERAPISTS / INDEPENDENT EXPERTS

Professional websites for people whose work is harder to explain than to trust.

A professional website should help the right client understand what you do, recognize their situation and decide whether a conversation makes sense.

This is not a digital CV and not a page full of generic authority language. It is for the moment when social profiles are doing the job of a website, a new practice needs a proper home or an old site no longer reflects the offer. We turn expertise, approach, proof and practical working details into a site that sounds like the person behind it. Clients often search for their problem before they know your professional category; the page should meet them at that first question.

THE REAL PROBLEM

Expertise is not automatically clear.

Clients may understand your title and still not know when to contact you, what the work feels like or why your approach is different from the next person's.

RECOGNITION

Start with the client's situation.

Specific problems and moments of decision are more useful than a broad list of capabilities.

TRUST

Use proof without turning into a trophy wall.

Credentials, operating history, process and real cases appear where they answer a visitor's uncertainty.

BOUNDARIES

Make fit and next steps explicit.

Who the work is for, what it is not, how it starts and how to contact you should not require detective work.

WHAT WE BUILD

A compact site with enough depth.

The goal is not the fewest possible words. It is the smallest useful structure that can carry the buying decision without flattening the work.

Positioning & narrative

A clear category, a human first screen and language that distinguishes the work without inventing a grand methodology.

Services & situations

One focused page or a small set of service pages, each with its own intent, evidence and next step.

Authority & contact

Relevant credentials, about material, case evidence, FAQs and a low-friction way to begin a conversation.

FAQ

Useful distinctions.

Professional sites are small, but the source material and trust requirements are not always simple.

Who is this service for?
Consultants, advisors, therapists, specialists and other independent experts whose work needs more explanation and trust than a social profile can provide.
Do I need finished copy before we start?
No. Positioning, structure and copy can be part of the project. Existing notes, profiles and conversations are useful source material.
Can the site support more than one service?
Yes, when the services belong to one coherent professional offer. If they serve very different audiences or buying decisions, the structure may need separate pages.
Will the website make claims about results?
Only claims supported by approved facts, credentials, examples or testimonials. We do not invent case studies, outcomes or authority signals.
