# Lead classification and 60–90 day measurement

This classification is internal. The public form stays open-ended and does not ask visitors to choose a service category.

## Lead categories

- `founder_mentoring_advisory` — founder mentoring or independent advisory.
- `business_growth_constraint` — growth plateau or operating constraint.
- `strategy_pivot` — strategy, business-model change or pivot.
- `founder_role_organization` — delegation, founder/CEO role or organization transition.
- `website_development` — focused landing page, launch page or compact business website.
- `professional_website` — website for a consultant, psychologist, mentor or independent expert.
- `other_unqualified` — unclear, outside scope or not yet qualified.

The source page supplies an initial direction only. A human confirms the category after reading the enquiry. Telegram and email clicks are `contact_intent`, not completed leads.

## KPI ladder

1. Non-brand impressions by query cluster and landing page.
2. Organic entrances by landing page and language.
3. Contact intent: successful form submit, Telegram or email click.
4. Qualified conversations, classified manually from delivered enquiries.
5. Paid engagements, connected to the originating landing page when evidence exists.

## Review cadence

- Days 1–14: verify indexing, hreflang, form delivery, consent and live events.
- Days 15–30: establish a baseline; do not optimize from isolated clicks.
- Days 31–60: review query-to-page fit and contact intent by page type.
- Days 61–90: compare qualified conversations and paid work by category. Improve existing pages before adding new near-duplicates.

Do not send private enquiry text to GA4. Exact attribution may be unavailable for outbound Telegram or email; record uncertainty rather than inventing a conversion.
