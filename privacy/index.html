PRIVACY

Analytics, not surveillance.

Lord Tuna uses website analytics to understand which pages and links are useful. We do not use the contact form to build advertising profiles or sell personal data.

Contact form

If you use Call Tuna, the name, contact detail and message you submit are sent through FormSubmit and forwarded to Lord Tuna by email. The information is used to reply to your enquiry and manage the potential project. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for up to 30 days.

Analytics

Google Analytics may be used to measure page views and actions such as project views, language changes, email clicks, Telegram clicks and successful contact-form submissions. Analytics should only start after consent where consent is required.
