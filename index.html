How large should the first implementation be?

Usually one high-value workflow with clear inputs, outputs and an owner. A focused first scope is easier to verify before expanding.

How quickly can you estimate the work?

An initial fit assessment follows a short task review. A reliable estimate comes after the sources, access, integrations and approval steps are understood.

What access is required?

Only access needed for the agreed scenario, ideally with the least permissions possible. Access is requested after the workflow and responsibilities are approved.

Are third-party services included?

No. Data providers, AI models, automation platforms and ad spend are approved and paid separately, without being hidden inside an implementation quote.

What happens after handover?

You receive the workflow, instructions and known limitations. Monitoring, maintenance and further iterations can be scoped as optional support.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. We can improve the research, routing and measurement workflow. Commercial outcomes still depend on the offer, audience, channel and follow-up.
