CASE STUDY / PSYCHOLOGY / PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

Petruseva.com

A professional psychology site that feels safe without becoming vague.

View live site ↗
THE BUSINESS

Anastasia Petruseva’s private psychology and Gestalt therapy practice for individual and couples work online.

THE QUESTION

How do you build trust and explain professional services without making the page either clinical and cold or full of generic wellbeing language?

THE LANGUAGE

Calm, specific and human. The language of the client’s lived situation first; professional credentials when they help reduce uncertainty.

THE POSITIONING

A place where a person can exhale, understand what is happening and find support without being pushed into a sales funnel.

THE STRUCTURE

A page starts
before Figma.

Emotional recognition first, then services and situations, professional grounding, approach and a low-pressure contact path.

WHAT WE DID

Thinking first.
Website second.

  • Business breakdown
  • Audience language
  • Positioning
  • Copy
  • Art direction
  • Responsive design
  • Development
  • SEO structure
THE RESULT

A compact professional site that balances warmth, trust, qualification and a clear path to contact.
