THE BUSINESS

Independent work with founders around difficult business decisions, role transitions, pivots and operating constraints.

THE QUESTION

How do you explain a service that sits between strategy, operating experience and founder reflection without calling it generic consulting or coaching?

THE LANGUAGE

Founder-to-founder. Specific, grounded and comfortable with ambiguity. No borrowed certainty, no motivational language.

THE POSITIONING

Founder Counterpart — an independent person who can think with the founder across the business, the decision and their own role.

THE STRUCTURE

A page starts
before Figma.

Start from recognizable founder situations, explain the working lens, show operating proof, then make the first conversation easy.

WHAT WE DID

Thinking first.
Website second.

  • Business breakdown
  • Audience language
  • Positioning
  • Copy
  • Information architecture
  • Art direction
  • Development
  • Technical SEO
THE RESULT

A site where the category becomes understandable through the founder’s actual situations instead of through a list of consulting packages.
