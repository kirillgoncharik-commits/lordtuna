THE BUSINESS

Game art, animation, iGaming production and co-development for studios that need flexible capacity and experienced delivery.

THE QUESTION

How can a new studio show senior operating experience without presenting earlier employers, companies or projects as current client work?

THE LANGUAGE

Direct, B2B and slightly unhinged. Clear about scope, review gates, AI use and what is illustrative rather than shipped client work.

THE POSITIONING

Flexible game-production support with experienced leadership, practical AI-assisted workflows and human review close to the work.

THE STRUCTURE

Credibility
